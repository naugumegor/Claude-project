## Outside in

PESTEL (the world) → five forces (the industry) → SWOT (our company). Example: a small metal 3D-printing bureau serving machine builders.

## PESTEL

Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, Legal. Keep only factors that move revenue or cost in 3–5 years, and say how.

```canvas
type: pestel
title: PESTEL for metal 3D printing
political: Import substitution programmes
economic: High interest rates delay customers' own capex
social: Shortage of additive-manufacturing engineers
technological: Printers and powders get cheaper, build volumes grow
environmental: Less scrap than machining from billet
legal: Aerospace and medical parts need process certification
```

## Five forces

Rivalry, threat of entrants, threat of substitutes, buyer power, supplier power. Stronger forces mean lower industry profits. Rate each force and justify it.

```canvas
type: porter
title: Five forces for metal 3D printing
rivalry: Medium, few players but more service bureaus
entrants: Medium, printers are expensive but leasing helps
substitutes: High, machining and casting win on simple parts
buyers: High, large plants and long tenders
suppliers: High, few powder and printer vendors
```

## SWOT and TOWS

Internal: strengths and weaknesses (relative to competitors, with numbers). External: opportunities and threats.

```canvas
type: swot
title: SWOT for metal 3D printing
strengths: Complex geometry without tooling; 5–7 day lead time; topology optimisation skills
weaknesses: Expensive above 100 parts; a single printer
opportunities: Replacing unavailable imported spares; turbine blade repair; implants
threats: Powder price growth; a large competitor with its own fleet
```

TOWS pairs the quadrants: SO (use strengths to seize opportunities), ST (use strengths against threats), WO (use opportunities to fix weaknesses), WT (defend). The output is 3–5 actions with owners and dates.

