## Terms (ISO 286)

Nominal size, upper/lower deviations (ES, EI for holes; es, ei for shafts), tolerance = upper − lower. Tolerance zone = letter + grade: H7, g6.

## IT grades

IT01…IT18, larger is coarser. For Ø30–50: IT6 16 µm, IT7 25 µm, IT8 39 µm, IT9 62 µm. Turning IT9–IT11, grinding IT6–IT7, lapping IT5 and finer. General tolerances are typically IT14 or ISO 2768-m.

## Fundamental deviations

Capitals for holes, lower case for shafts. H: lower deviation 0; h: upper 0. Shafts a–g give clearance, js–n transition, p–z interference with an H hole.

## Hole basis

Use H holes (usually H7) and vary the shaft: holes are made with fixed-size tools and checked with plug gauges. Shaft basis (h) for ground bar stock, bearing outer rings and keys.

## Fits

| Fit | Type | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `H7/h6` | sliding | precise location, hand assembly |
| `H7/g6` | close running | spools, plungers |
| `H7/f7` | running | plain bearings |
| `H9/d9` | free running | high speed, thermal expansion |
| `H7/js6` | transition | pulleys, frequent disassembly |
| `H7/k6` | transition | gears on keyed shafts |
| `H7/n6` | transition | rarely disassembled |
| `H7/p6` | light press | bushings |
| `H7/r6` | medium press | gear rims |
| `H7/s6`, `H7/u7` | press / heavy | no key, shrink fit |

Ø40 H7/p6: hole $^{+0.025}_{0}$, shaft $^{+0.042}_{+0.026}$, interference 0.001–0.042 mm.

Rolling bearings: specify only shaft and housing, e.g. rotating inner ring on k6/js6, stationary outer ring in H7.

## Flashcards
