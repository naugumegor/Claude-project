## Standards

ESKD (GOST 2.xxx) and ISO 128/129/5456. Both use **first-angle projection** (the US uses third-angle).

## Sheets and lines

A0 841×1189 … A3 297×420, A4 210×297. Frame 20 mm left, 5 mm elsewhere; title block 185×55 in the bottom right.

Thick continuous: visible outlines. Thin continuous: dimensions, hatching, leaders. Dashed: hidden edges. Thin chain: centre lines. Wavy: break lines. Dimensions are always true size whatever the scale.

## Views

Front (principal) view shows the most; first angle puts the top view below and the left view on the right. Auxiliary and partial views as needed, as few as possible.

## Sections

A **section view** shows the cut and everything behind it; a **cut section** only the cut face. Full, half (symmetric parts: half view, half section, section on the right), offset, aligned, local (broken-out). Shafts, bolts, keys, balls and thin ribs are not hatched when cut lengthwise. Hatch metal at 45°, same on all views of a part.

## Dimensions

Enough to make and inspect the part, each once, in mm. Keep ≥ 10 mm from the outline, ≥ 7 mm between rows. Ø, R, □, S symbols. Never close a dimension chain. Dimension from functional and manufacturing datums. Chamfers "2×45°".

## Threads

External: major diameter thick, minor thin; internal: the reverse; end view shows a 3/4 thin arc. M20, M20×1.5, M20×1.5LH, M20-6g.

## Flashcards
