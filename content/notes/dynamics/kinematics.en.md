## Point

$\vec v = \dot{\vec r}$, $\vec a = \ddot{\vec r}$. Along a path: $a_\tau = dv/dt$ changes speed, $a_n = v^2/\rho$ changes direction. Uniform circular motion still has $a_n = v^2/R$.

## Rotation about a fixed axis

$\omega = \dot\varphi$, $\varepsilon = \dot\omega$; at radius $r$: $v = \omega r$, $a_\tau = \varepsilon r$, $a_n = \omega^2 r$. $\omega = \pi n/30$ for $n$ in rpm. No-slip drive: $\omega_1r_1 = \omega_2r_2$, ratio $u = z_2/z_1$.

## Planar motion

$\vec v_B = \vec v_A + \vec\omega\times\vec{AB}$. Projections of velocities onto the line joining two points are equal.

**Instantaneous centre of zero velocity** $P$: $v_M = \omega\cdot PM$, $\vec v_M\perp PM$. Find it at the intersection of normals to two known velocities; for a rolling wheel it is the contact point (the top moves at $2v$); parallel non-perpendicular velocities mean instantaneous translation.

## Relative motion

$\vec v = \vec v_e + \vec v_r$, $\vec a = \vec a_e + \vec a_r + \vec a_c$ with **Coriolis** $\vec a_c = 2\vec\omega_e\times\vec v_r$, zero for translating frames or $\vec v_r\parallel\vec\omega_e$.
